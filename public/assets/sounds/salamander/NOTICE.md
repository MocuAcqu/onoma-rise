# Salamander Grand Piano samples

These MP3 files are a reduced web subset of **Salamander Grand Piano**, a
Yamaha C5 recorded by Alexander Holm.

- Original work: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
- Web-formatted source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
